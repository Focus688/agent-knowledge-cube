# Z: Competitor Analysis Template

## 竞争对手识别 / Competitor Identification
- **Direct competitors** — 相同用户需求 / same user needs, same product category
- **Indirect competitors** — 替代解决方案 / alternative solutions, different category
- **Emerging threats** — 初创公司跨界 / startups entering adjacent space
- **Legacy alternatives** — 传统方法 / status quo behaviors

## Feature Comparison Grid
| Feature | Us | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|---------|----|--------------|--------------|
| Core feature ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Pricing model | Freemium | Subscription | Per-seat |

## Pricing Comparison
- Tier structure, per-user cost, annual discount, hidden fees
- Value ratio: features per dollar

## SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths** — 内部优势 / internal differentiators
- **Weaknesses** — 内部短板 / gaps vs competitors
- **Opportunities** — 外部机会 / market trends, underserved segments
- **Threats** — 外部威胁 / competitor moves, regulatory changes

## Market Positioning Map
X-axis: Price (low→high), Y-axis: Feature depth (basic→advanced). Plot competitors to find whitespace.

## 更新节奏 / Update Cadence
- Ongoing tracking: monthly signal scan (funding, product launches, hiring)
- Full deep-dive: quarterly with team review
